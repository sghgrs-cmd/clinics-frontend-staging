01 The Dentist

Dr. Nick Riccio

Hinsdale Orthodontics & Pediatric Dentistry has offered high-quality orthodontic treatment in a friendly, welcoming environment for more than 70 years. We serve the amazing families of Hinsdale and the surrounding areas of Brookfield, Oak Brook, LaGrange, Darien, and Western Springs.

Because our office has been in practice for over 70 years, we have a pretty good idea of how faces will change over time. We offer Clear Aligners, AcceleDent®, Self-ligating Damon® braces, lingual braces, and sealants to provide an extra layer of protection for vulnerable back teeth.

I love working in orthodontics because I'm constantly meeting new people. You never get a second chance to make a first impression.

05 Standards

Built on precision.

Digital planning

Every case starts on screen, not in the mouth. A digital scan shows the plan before a single tool touches a tooth.

Precision over guesswork

Measurements come from the scan, not from memory. What is proposed is what gets made, without chairside improvising.

Records that last

Your scan, photographs, and plan stay on file, so a future visit builds on what is already known about your bite.
